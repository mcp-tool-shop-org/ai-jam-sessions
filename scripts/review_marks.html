Listening review

Play the song. When you hear a stutter or anything else wrong, press M. The song pauses so you can say what you heard; press Enter to keep listening. You don't need to be quick or exact: a mark looks back over the second before you pressed. Marks are saved in this browser as you go. Tell Claude when you're done.
